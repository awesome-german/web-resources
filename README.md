## Dictionaries

- [German-English Dictionary App](https://german-english-dictionary.devtheworld.jp/) - For scientifically-efficient modern learning.
- [Duden](https://www.duden.de/) - The authoritative German dictionary and grammar reference.
- [DWDS](https://www.dwds.de/) - Digital Dictionary of the German Language with historical usage examples.
- [Leo.org](https://www.leo.org/) - Comprehensive multilingual dictionary with forums.
- [Linguee](https://www.linguee.com/) - Dictionary with real-world translation examples from bilingual texts.
- [Reverso Context](https://context.reverso.net/translation/) - Translations in context from authentic sources.

## Language Learning Platforms

- [Deutsche Welle - Learn German](https://www.dw.com/en/learn-german/s-2469) - Free German courses from beginner to advanced levels.
- [Goethe-Institut](https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/ueb.html) - Exercises and materials from the official German cultural institute.
- [LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org/) - Grammar and spell checker for German and other languages.
- [Easy German](https://www.easygerman.org/) - Community-driven resources with videos and learning materials.

## Grammar Resources

- [Canoo.net](https://www.canoo.net/) - Comprehensive German grammar and word formation reference.
- [German Grammar by Nancy Thuleen](http://www.nthuleen.com/teach.html) - Detailed grammar explanations and exercises.
- [Hammer's German Grammar and Usage](https://www.deutsche-grammatik.com/) - Online reference for German grammar.

## Digital Libraries and Archives

- [Gutenberg-DE](https://www.gutenberg.org/browse/languages/de) - Public domain German literature in digital format.
- [Deutsches Textarchiv (DTA)](http://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/) - Digital archive of German texts from 1600-1900.
- [Zeno.org](http://www.zeno.org/) - Full texts of German literature, philosophy, and cultural history.
- [Europeana](https://www.europeana.eu/en) - Digital platform for European cultural heritage, including German materials.

## News and Media

- [Tagesschau](https://www.tagesschau.de/) - Germany's main news portal (ARD).
- [Deutsche Welle](https://www.dw.com/de/) - International broadcaster with news in German and other languages.
- [Zeit Online](https://www.zeit.de/) - Online edition of the weekly newspaper Die Zeit.
- [Spiegel Online](https://www.spiegel.de/) - News and current affairs magazine.
- [Nachrichtenleicht](https://www.nachrichtenleicht.de/) - News in simplified German for learners.

## Literature and Texts

- [Projekt Gutenberg-DE](https://www.projekt-gutenberg.org/) - German language literature collection.
- [Lyrikline](https://www.lyrikline.org/de/startseite) - Contemporary poetry in German with audio recordings.
- [Märchenatlas](https://www.maerchenatlas.de/) - Fairy tales and folk tales in German.

## Audio and Podcasts

- [Slow German](https://www.slowgerman.com/) - Podcasts for German learners with transcripts.
- [GermanPod101](https://www.germanpod101.com/) - Audio and video lessons for all levels.
- [Coffee Break German](https://coffeebreaklanguages.com/coffeebreakgerman/) - Learn German through podcast lessons.
- [Deutschlandfunk](https://www.deutschlandfunk.de/) - Public radio with a wide range of cultural and news programs.

## Video Resources

- [Easy German YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/c/EasyGerman) - Street interviews and cultural content.
- [Learn German with Anja](https://www.youtube.com/c/LearnGermanWithAnja) - Grammar lessons and vocabulary.
- [ARD Mediathek](https://www.ardmediathek.de/) - Online media library of German public television.
- [ZDF Mediathek](https://www.zdf.de/) - Media library of Germany's second public broadcaster.

## Cultural Resources

- [Goethe-Institut](https://www.goethe.de/en/index.html) - Promoting German language and culture worldwide.
- [Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek](https://www.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/) - German Digital Library with cultural and scientific heritage.
- [Bundeszentrale für politische Bildung](https://www.bpb.de/) - Federal Agency for Civic Education resources.

## Academic Resources

- [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/) - Academic journals and books (many German studies articles).
- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) - Search for scholarly literature in German.
- [Academia.edu](https://www.academia.edu/) - Academic papers and research in German studies.
- [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/) - Academic social network with German language research.

## Tools and Utilities

- [Verbformen.de](https://www.verbformen.de/) - German verb conjugation tool.
- [Dict.cc](https://www.dict.cc/) - Collaborative dictionary with extensive vocabulary.
- [Forvo](https://forvo.com/languages/de/) - Pronunciation guide with native speakers.
- [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show_all_in/deu/none) - Example sentences in German with translations.
